Bird's-eye point picker

Open calib/bev_<side>_undistorted.png, click the centre of each floor marker, and type its floor position in metres from the camera's ground point (fwd = toward the car's front, rear negative; out = away from the car, positive). Then copy or download the text and save it on the car as calib/bev_<side>_points.txt. Nothing leaves your computer.
